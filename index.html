About me

You can call me Victor. A while back I used to go by Lyrica and DeathScythe, so if you see these names around, yeah, it's me.

I'm an artist, musician, gamer, MUGEN author, modder, streamer and all-around content creator, from Brazil. I'm pretty friendly, so don't be shy to reach out. Just don't come ask me for money!

If you're curious, you can find more information in the About Me section.
